# Entropy Engine

Nothing inside SilentNode is permanent by default.

Every idea, node, project, and connection has a living entropy state.

## How Entropy Works

When an entity receives no attention, interaction, or connection activity, its entropy begins to rise.

Entropy manifests visually:
- colors desaturate and fade,
- edges and connections grow thinner and more translucent,
- the node's visual mass decreases,
- particle activity around the entity slows,
- light emission dims toward silence.

At critical entropy levels:
- the entity enters a pre-collapse state,
- it emits a faint visual pulse — a final breath,
- if still unattended, it dissolves into a Ghost state.

## Entropy Is Not Deletion

Entropy collapse does not destroy.

It transforms:
- the entity becomes a Temporal Ghost,
- its structure is preserved in a dormant layer,
- its connections become invisible but intact,
- it can be fully restored at any time.

## Entropy Reversal

Attention reverses entropy.

When a user interacts with, references, or connects to an entity:
- entropy immediately begins to reverse,
- color and mass return,
- connections regain strength,
- the entity re-enters the living layer.

## Entropy Visualization Scale

Each entity exists on an entropy spectrum:

- **Vibrant** — recently active, fully alive,
- **Cooling** — activity slowing, subtle desaturation,
- **Fading** — low engagement, visible decay,
- **Crystallizing** — near-collapse, structural fragmentation,
- **Ghost** — dissolved into the dormant layer.

## Why Entropy Exists

Entropy creates honest reflection.

The user does not need reminders or notifications.

They simply observe which parts of their universe are dying.

This awareness naturally guides attention without external pressure.

# Silent Contracts

Not all commitments are spoken.

Many of the most important obligations a person carries exist only in intention — never written, never declared, but felt.

Silent Contracts are SilentNode's system for making these invisible obligations visible.

---

## What Is a Silent Contract

A Silent Contract is a structural entity that the system derives from observed behavior rather than explicit user declaration.

It represents:

- a goal implied by repeated behavior but never formally stated,
- a deadline the user set for themselves and then avoided,
- an intention expressed in a past journal entry that has gone unaddressed,
- a project that was started with high energy but has been quietly abandoned,
- a recurring thought that appears regularly but has never been acted upon,
- a connection the user has approached multiple times but never completed.

---

## How Silent Contracts Are Detected

The system observes patterns across time:

- **Repeated approach without engagement** — the user navigates near a node or project repeatedly but does not open it. This signals avoidance of an unresolved obligation.
- **Journal declaration without follow-through** — a thought or intention recorded in the journal that has generated no corresponding node or activity.
- **High-gravity nodes with no output** — nodes that receive significant attention but have not evolved or produced connections. The user thinks about this but does nothing.
- **Temporal decay of high-priority entities** — a node that was once at the center of the universe but has drifted outward without resolution.
- **Orphaned project structures** — projects that have architecture and nodes but whose activity trails have gone cold.

---

## How Silent Contracts Appear

Silent Contracts are rendered as distinct visual entities:

- they appear as **faint structural outlines** — not fully materialized, not fully absent,
- they exist at the periphery of their related cluster,
- they pulse with a slow, quiet rhythm — a heartbeat of unresolved potential,
- they carry no color — only a translucent structural form,
- they cast a subtle shadow over their related nodes,
- they do not demand attention — they simply persist.

---

## Interacting with Silent Contracts

The user has three options when confronting a Silent Contract:

### Fulfill

The user commits to addressing the contract.

The contract becomes a living node.

It enters the active graph with full gravitational and connective properties.

Its visual form solidifies and gains color.

### Release

The user consciously chooses to dissolve the contract.

It does not collapse into a Ghost — it performs a distinct dissolution animation:

- a slow exhale of light,
- the structural outline fades,
- a single temporal marker is placed in the archaeology layer recording the moment of release.

Release is not failure.

It is conscious closure.

### Leave

The user ignores the contract.

It continues to exist at the periphery.

It gains no additional pressure.

But it remains — patient and present.

---

## Silent Contract Awareness

The system presents a Silent Contract Overview — a dedicated view showing:

- all currently detected contracts,
- their estimated age — how long the underlying obligation has existed,
- their intensity — derived from how frequently the related area has been approached,
- their cluster — which project, theme, or life area they belong to.

No recommendations.

No urgency scores.

Only honest visibility.

---

## Why Silent Contracts Exist

Traditional productivity systems create obligations externally.

Tasks are assigned. Deadlines are imposed. Reminders fire.

SilentNode takes a different position.

The obligations that matter most are the ones a person already carries internally.

Silent Contracts surface what the user already knows but has not yet faced.

The system does not add pressure.

It removes the comfort of forgetting.

- they carry gravity — approaching events increase in visual mass,
- they cast shadows backward in time — preparation activity is linked to the event,
- they create anticipation fields — nearby nodes in the graph become more active as events approach,
- they leave impressions forward in time — post-event reflections are linked to the original node.

## Intelligent Event Awareness

The system observes patterns and develops temporal intelligence:

- detecting recurring cycles — weekly reviews, monthly planning, seasonal shifts,
- recognizing preparation behavior — when the user naturally begins working toward a deadline,
- identifying procrastination signatures — when approach behavior is absent before a known event,

## Notification Philosophy

SilentNode does not interrupt.

Notifications do not fire as alerts.

Instead:

- approaching events cause their Temporal Nodes to pulse more intensely,
- the surrounding environment subtly shifts as an event nears,
- the user's attention is drawn — never grabbed,
- critical events can be configured to trigger a gentle environmental transformation rather than a notification.
